Brand Intelligence Dashboard · Design Exploration

The dashboard that decides whether a brand renews. Three directions for the "do I renew?" moment: a hero-metric layout, a newsroom feed, and a weekly email digest. Same data; different ways of presenting "you're winning."

Direction A · Hero metric layout

Lead with the single most-asked-for number: how many creators recommend you. Trend, then category context, then social proof. First 30 seconds = wow.

Why this works: The hero number is what every brand on a call says first: "How many creators talk about us?" Trend gives the "we're winning" beat. Stats row covers the secondary asks (rank, ROI, reach). Quote provides emotional payoff. Creator grid gives them ammunition for their next team meeting.

Critical: The "+18%" needs to be true and meaningful. If we surface a fake-feeling number, we lose trust fast. Suggest: compute weekly, smooth over 4 weeks, hide if dataset too small.

Why this works: Every card is a decision the brand could make. The highlight at the top + 4 follow-on cards give a "5 things you should know" feel. Each card has its own micro-CTA. Maps cleanly to a weekly cadence.

When this wins: Better for engaged brands who log in often. Risk: empty state. If a brand has a slow week, the feed looks empty. Need a fallback for low-activity weeks ("nothing major this week, but you're holding steady at...").

Why this works: Lands in their inbox without requiring login. Forwardable to their CMO. Each section maps to a question their team is asking. The rank visualization is the killer feature — they can show this slide as-is in a board meeting.

Combination strategy: Build A (dashboard) and C (email) as one product. The email is a digest of the dashboard. They reinforce each other — email pulls brand back to the dashboard for the deep dive.

Recommendation

Ship Direction A as the brand-portal home, paired with Direction C as the Monday digest. Direction B's newsroom pattern becomes a "What's New" section inside Direction A — fewer separate surfaces to maintain, same content beats.

The email digest does the re-engagement work; the dashboard does the on-call "show your CMO this number" work. Together they solve both the "wow on signup" and the "compel them to renew" problems.
