Product doc · Brand Intelligence Dashboard

The "do I renew?" surface. The single feature most likely to make a brand expand spend. Bundles ID 8, 12, 100 (UGC discovery), 115 (competitor analysis).

The opportunity

Brands today renew based on a campaign's anecdotal feel. We have the data to show them "X creators recommend you · top 3 quotes · trending +12% vs last month · #2 in 'clean beauty' category vs Tower28 #1, Saie #3". That single screen is the difference between a brand sticking around for one campaign vs. expanding to ongoing spend.

Target user

The brand-side decision maker — typically a founder, CMO, or marketing director. They land on this dashboard on their portal home and either get a story they can take to their team, or they don't.

v1 scope (in)

  • Recommendation count — how many Benable creators have recommended this brand, all-time + last 30 days. Sparkline.
  • Top quote highlights — 3–5 best quotes pulled from rec lists, manually approvable.
  • Category benchmark — "you rank #X in {category}" with top 3 competitors visible (anonymized counts unless you're #1).
  • Mention trend — last 90 days of organic mentions on social (depends on cluster 01 ID 96).
  • Creator highlights — top 5 creators who mention you, with content thumbnails.

v1 scope (out)

  • Sentiment analysis (defer to v2 once we have NLP).
  • Cross-platform reach numbers (Instagram impressions etc.) — too noisy without API access.
  • Direct messaging from this view; it's read-only for v1.

Success metrics

  • Brand portal weekly active brands ≥ 60% of paying brands (today: estimated < 30%).
  • Renewal conversation rate: % of brands that bring up dashboard data on their renewal call (qualitative).
  • Time to "first wow" on signup ≤ 2 minutes — measured by first dashboard load + dwell.

Open product decisions

D-04 · Should category rankings be visible to all brands, or premium-tier only?
Option A · All brands see anonymized rank, premium sees who ranks above them
Creates a clear premium-tier value (transparency on competition). Drives upsells. Recommended.
Option B · All brands see full data
Maximally helpful. Burns the chance to monetize the insight tier.
Option C · Premium only, no free version
Most monetizable. Hurts free-tier stickiness; may slow word-of-mouth from brands telling each other "look at this dashboard."
D-05 · Where does this dashboard live in the brand-portal IA?
Option A · It IS the brand-portal home
Dashboard becomes the front door. Forces every brand to see their data on every login. Strongest "wow" placement.
Option B · A new tab "Insights" alongside Campaigns
Less disruptive to existing IA. Lower discovery — brands have to choose to look.
Option C · A weekly emailed digest, not a tab
Pushes engagement instead of pulling. Best at re-engagement. Weakest at "wow."

Phasing

  1. v0: Static "snapshot" PDF auto-generated weekly per brand. Validates content + headline metrics with real brands.
  2. v1: Live dashboard with the four blocks above. Powers the IA decision (D-05).
  3. v1.5: Add competitor analysis (ID 115).
  4. v2: Sentiment, trend forecasting, automated alerts.

Open question on Brand CRM (ID 20)

D-06 · Brand CRM — build internal vs. wire up Attio/HubSpot/Salesforce?
Option A · Build proprietary CRM (current direction)
Custom-fit to brand-portal data. No vendor lock-in. ~3 months to feature parity with off-the-shelf for our use case. Tony's spec already exists.
Option B · Attio + custom data sync
Fastest to actually-useful. Attio's data model is flexible enough for ICP scoring + outreach moments. Sienna can use it day one. We own the brand data sync, not the CRM. Recommended unless there's a specific reason proprietary is better.
Option C · HubSpot Sales Hub
Most mature for sequencing. Heavier; less flexible data model. Higher cost.

Note: this question may already be settled in Tony's mind. If proprietary is locked in, the actual decision is just "what's in CRM v1?" — and we should write that scope, not re-litigate the build/buy.
