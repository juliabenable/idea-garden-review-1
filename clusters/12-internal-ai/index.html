Recommendation framing

This cluster shouldn't crowd consumer/brand product work. Three filters for what's worth doing:

1. Finish prototype review tool (ID 134)

85% built. The remaining 15% probably converts more eng-team value than starting any new internal tool. Single highest-ROI in this cluster.

2. Design system constraints (ID 133)

Direct cause of divergent components Tony flagged. Prevents future technical debt at the rate-limiting point — prototypes shipping with new UI. Prerequisite to scaling AI-built prototypes.

3. AI creator cards rollout (ID 132)

Already in test with Des. Concrete loop: AI generates → human accept/deny → reduces 50% rejection rate from Cluster 04. Connects to product, not internal-only.

What to park

AI Feedback Agent in Tony's voice (ID 139), Figma → mobile codebase (ID 137), and similar speculative agents. They're "would be cool" rather than "would unblock something." Add to the parking lot, revisit if internal-tools capacity opens.

Open product decisions

D-23 · Should "internal AI tools" have its own owner / track, or stay opportunistic?
Option A · Opportunistic — when a Build-a-thon surfaces something concrete, build it
Avoids creating a function nobody asked for. Lets internal tools follow real pain. Recommended.
Option B · Dedicate one part-time eng to "internal tools team"
Faster delivery on multiple internal tools. Costs eng capacity that could go to consumer/brand. Reasonable in 6 months once the team grows.
Option C · Outsource to AI / contractors
Lowest direct cost. Highest quality risk for Benny-style tools that touch prod data.
