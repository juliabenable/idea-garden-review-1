Design

Product doc · "Listen sprint"

Bundle the QoL fixes into one sprint with a clear theme. Quality wins ship together so creators see the change.

The pitch

Each fix is small. Bundling them under "We listened" gives the team a story to tell creators, and gives engineering one well-shaped sprint instead of 14 tickets sprinkled across two months.

v1 scope

  • Notifications: Push (ID 23) and email (ID 85) for invites with creator + brand names in subject. Auto-text deadline reminder when package arrives (ID 82).
  • Address confirmation visibility (ID 86): Promote shipping step to its own card; keep visible after confirmation.
  • List optimization indicator specificity (ID 28): "Missing: short description, image, why-you-love-it" instead of just a yellow dot.
  • Brand category preferences (ID 88, 90, 92): Single settings screen — block categories, opt-in collab types, opt-in brand values. Settings power both creator filtering and brand-side matching.
  • Hashtag prompt (ID 93, see decision): Optional helper before publish.

Open product decisions

D-11 · Hashtag automation (ID 93) — helper or hard gate?
Option A · Soft prompt before publish ("Did you mean to add #brand?")
Helps creators who forget without policing. Lowest friction, lowest creator-pissed-off risk. Recommended.
Option B · Hard gate; can't mark complete without required hashtag
Best compliance signal for brands. Costs creator goodwill — feels surveillance-y for what is ultimately their post.
Option C · Brand picks per campaign (soft / hard / off)
Most flexible. More config UI to build. Deferrable to v2.

UI exploration · invite notification

The notification creators receive when invited to a campaign. Three formats — same data, very different "missability."

Option A · Rich push with brand mark Recommended
Soluna invited you 2m ago
@hanahbae · You're a fit for "Clean Beauty Spring '26"
Gifted · 2 products · 5 days · accept by Fri
Why this: Both names in title. Key terms ("gifted, 5 days") in the snippet. Two CTAs: View / Accept. iOS rich notification format. Hard to miss.
Option B · Plain push (status quo improved)
@hanahbae · Soluna invited you
"Clean Beauty Spring '26" — accept by Friday
Why this: Smaller, taps to open. Lowest implementation cost. Misses the "scan + decide" moment. Recommended only as a fallback for older OS.
Option C · In-app drawer with countdown
NEW INVITE
Soluna · Clean Beauty
Accept in 2d 14h
YOUR CAMPAIGNS
2 active
Why this: Pinned drawer at top of home — hard to miss if creator opens the app. Doesn't help if creator never opens the app. Best paired with Option A push.
Recommendation: Ship Option A (rich push) + Option C (in-app drawer) as one feature. They cover the two miss patterns: "didn't open the app for 2 days" and "missed the lock-screen banner."
