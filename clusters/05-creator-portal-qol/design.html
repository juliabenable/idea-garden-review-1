Creator QoL · Design Exploration

Two surfaces. The creator's "What I want" preferences screen (consolidates 4 scattered settings) and three takes on the campaign invite notification — the Leea Sarvela missed-invite problem.

Single screen consolidating collab types (ID 90), categories (ID 88), brand values (ID 92), and blocks. Same data drives matching and onboarding.

Why this works: Tap-and-toggle interaction throughout. Color-coded by intent (brand purple for positive preferences, red for blocks, green for values). Maps 1:1 to brand-side filters in cluster 02 — what brands filter by IS what creators set here.

Behavior: Toggle is instant (no Save until you leave). Each change immediately affects which campaigns surface — show a toast like "now showing 38 campaigns" right after a toggle to make the cause-effect visible.

Recommendation
