Research doc · Local Audience Verification Experiment

Don't build local matching. Run a 2-week experiment that answers one question: can we prove a creator's audience overlaps with a target zip code well enough that a local brand pays for it?

The question

Local matching only matters if a brand will pay for "this creator can drive customers to my restaurant in zip 90004." Without that, every idea in this cluster collapses. Audience-location data exists in app, but we haven't tested whether it's strong enough to underwrite a paid local campaign.

Experiment design

  1. Pick one vertical: restaurants. Strongest signal (Jessica). Lowest risk for the brand (small dollar amounts, comp meals).
  2. Pick 3 cities + 5 creators each. Use location data we already collect. Aim for "definitely local" creators (lived 1+ year, posts about local stuff).
  3. Pitch one local brand per city with a specific spec: "5 creators with X% audience in your zip code, hosted meal of $200, 1 story + 1 reel each, you pay $500 if accepted."
  4. Track the actual outcome: Reservations / mentions / new orders attributable to the campaign. Use unique reservation links + creator reach reports.

Decision criteria after experiment

  • If brands pay AND outcome is measurable: green-light a Local Restaurants v1 with reservation tracking (ID 120).
  • If brands pay but outcome is muddy: the wallet exists but our verification doesn't — invest in audience-location signal first (ID 121).
  • If brands don't pay: park the cluster. Move on. Resist the urge to "fix" with marketing.
