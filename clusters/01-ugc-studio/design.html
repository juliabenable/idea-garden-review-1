UGC Studio · Design Exploration

Three surfaces in depth: the brand-side license transaction, the brand-side asset marketplace (browse-to-license), and the creator-side rate-setting opt-in. Each has 2–3 design directions to compare.

Surface 1 · License transaction (brand side)

When a brand is viewing a specific creator asset and wants to license it. The 3-click moment that has to feel like Airbnb instant-book, not a procurement process.

Why this works: The asset is hero. Tiers sit beside it, scannable in one glance, active state visible. Creator info is small but present (trust signal). The "Hanah gets X" line is the empathy beat — brands like seeing it; creators feel like the platform is on their side too.

The risk: Three tier cards may feel like a lot to read. Mitigation: track which tier brands actually pick; if 85% pick "Paid 30 days," promote it visually and demote the others.

Why this works: Pinterest-grid scales — visual scanning over reading. Filter rail handles power-user discovery. Each tile shows the price for "Paid 30 days" so brands can budget at-a-glance. Clicking a tile drops them into Direction A above.
